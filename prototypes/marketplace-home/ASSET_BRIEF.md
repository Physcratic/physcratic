# Guest landing media brief

The page is intentionally composed around substantial image and video areas. Keep the reserved space until assets are ready.
Hero headline: **For those who want to win.** This exact owner-selected motto is approved.

| Asset | Composition and purpose | Suggested source format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Instructor portraits 01–03 | Editorial upper-body portraits with clear faces and room for a paired competition mark. Use consistent lighting/cropping, not celebrity-style name cards | Portrait 3:4 originals, ideally 1200px or taller; transparent cutouts optional |
| Competition marks | Small, clear competition logos paired to the relevant instructor; no names or lengthy award lists on the hero | Accurate SVG or high-resolution transparent source |
| Teaching excerpt | Instructor explains one idea/problem while their working is visible. A meaningful excerpt, not a promotional montage | 16:9 video, poster frame, captions and transcript |
| Annotated lesson | Readable crop of actual equations, diagrams, reasoning and a follow-up prompt | Landscape 4:3 or 16:10 screenshot, at least 1200px wide |
| Six subject covers | Distinct artwork for Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, AI and Informatics. Cohesive palette and style across subjects | 4:3 artwork, ideally 1200×900 or larger |

The prototype uses CSS silhouettes and labelled placeholders, not fabricated people or stock photos claiming to be instructors.
The screenshot drawing is illustrative. Video controls open a reserved area and never claim a recording is playing.
Current space allocation: hero portrait composition roughly 500px tall on desktop; teaching video roughly 16:9 with substantial minimum height; screenshot overlaps its lower-right area; subject covers use a three-column desktop grid and two columns on narrow screens.
Do not fill reserved space with more copy just because media is missing.

Before actual media is incorporated:
- Confirm permission to use identifiable portraits, lesson material and marks.
- Associate each competition mark accurately with the instructor. Do not imply the organisation endorses Physcratic.
- Keep detailed credentials on profiles/course pages. Provide meaningful accessible identification with real media.
- Provide alt text for informative images, empty alt for purely decorative art, and keyboard-accessible video controls with captions/transcript.
- Compress responsive images and defer below-fold media so the visual layout remains usable on mobile.
- Do not autoplay audio or invent reviews, learning outcomes or author relationships.
