## Privacy Notice proposal

### Author credit on community problems

> If you agree to author credit in Problem Studio, Physcratic uses your chosen username to credit you on all your published community problems, including problems you published before you agreed. That choice also applies to problems you publish later. Credit uses your username. Your account name, email address and profile display name are private for this purpose. A profile link is available only when your profile is public.
>
> We store the version of the disclosure you agreed to, when you agreed and, if you withdraw, when you withdrew. You can agree to credit with a private profile. If you deactivate your profile, separately agreed credit can still show your username on problems. Problem Studio has a separate option to withdraw author credit.
>
> Withdrawing author credit removes your credited username from new reads of your problems. The problems remain published and your profile keeps its own publication status. People who previously saw or copied your credit may retain a copy. If we delete your account, we remove your author-credit record. A problem may remain online under the account-deletion rules with a generic deleted-author credit.

For the existing “Hide your profile” section, keep the promise that deactivation hides the public profile and the name derived from profile publication. Add a separate sentence that tells authors where to withdraw independently agreed credit. Publish this change only after the owner reviews the disclosure and accepts the Batch 2 implementation.
