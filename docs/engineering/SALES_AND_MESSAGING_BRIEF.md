# Physcratic sales and messaging brief

11 November 2026. Read this before changing commercial page copy or composition.
This document records the sales reasoning; GUEST_HOMEPAGE_LAYOUT.md records the page arrangement. Do not make the owner direct every section removal or addition.

## Working method

1. Identify the visitor's task, current understanding and next commercial decision.
2. Identify the alternatives they actually consider, including continuing without buying.
3. Connect our capabilities to a benefit they care about.
4. Supply the kind of evidence needed for that benefit.
5. Resolve the objections relevant to this step.
6. Offer a clear next action with an accurate expectation of what follows.
7. Only then arrange copy, media and components.

Start with the customer and the offer, not a standard list of landing-page sections.
Use established frameworks to make decisions. Do not repeatedly tell the owner every choice is a hypothesis or ask them to micromanage routine details.
Ask for missing business facts when they change the truth of the offer. Do not invent those facts or customer quotations.

## The buyer and decision

Primary homepage visitor: a student seeking better preparation for a competition, who may know the competition but not Physcratic.
A parent or sponsor may be the payer and need additional purchasing/quality information. Do not force a universal student/parent journey.
Possible alternatives to investigate: free problem archives, videos, school clubs, another course and live tutoring. These are working examples, not a researched customer shortlist.
The commercial destination is suitable paid learning. Homepage success is qualified progression toward a suitable offer, not time spent scrolling.

## Translate assets into reasons to buy

| Asset/capability | Benefit to communicate | Evidence required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Competition-experienced instructors | Learn how experienced competitors approach unfamiliar problems | Accurate credentials plus an actual explanation showing teaching |
| Structured courses | Understand what to study and practise next | Syllabus, prerequisites, sequence and sample lesson |
| Worked examples and practice | Follow reasoning, then try applying it | Real lesson/product footage and representative problems |
| Multiple competition fields | Find relevant preparation in the intended area | Searchable actual inventory, accurate competition-course mapping |
| Coaching | Get personal explanation and feedback when independent study is insufficient | Actual class format, instructor fit and enquiry process |
| Arena | Try useful free physics practice and keep engaging | Actual working product, not only a screenshot |

Do not claim every competitor lacks these capabilities. The exact combination and quality must be demonstrated.
Medals support competition expertise. They do not establish teaching quality, student outcomes or a guaranteed win.

## Message sequence for the guest homepage

1. **Recognise the goal.** Keep the owner-selected motto: For those who want to win.
2. **Explain the offer immediately.** The supporting sentence must say what is sold, who teaches and how learning helps. Never expect the motto or logo rail to do that work alone.
3. **Establish relevant expertise.** Editorial portraits and accurately associated competition marks support the offer without a dense biography.
4. **Confirm relevance.** Competition lookup should reveal actual coverage, not merely list famous names.
5. **Demonstrate the learning mechanism.** Show explanation, worked reasoning and practice. Explain why these help, rather than listing video/PDF features.
6. **Offer the right alternative format.** Coaching serves a need for personal support. Arena serves immediate independent practice. Keep paid course discovery the dominant commercial path.
7. **Ask for an appropriate next commitment.** Explore courses takes the visitor to suitability assessment, not directly to an account wall or a surprise enquiry form.

The current page order remains owner-approved. This is the reasoning each section must deliver within that order, not permission for an unrelated rewrite.

## Handle objections where they arise

| Buyer question | Best answer/evidence | Location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Is this for my competition? | Actual coverage and useful match/no-match result | Coverage and catalogue |
| Are these people credible? | Correct competition credentials | Hero and instructor details |
| Can they explain it to someone at my level? | Sample teaching, intended learner and prerequisites | Teaching preview and course detail |
| What will I actually get? | Syllabus, lesson format, practice and support included | Course detail |
| Will I have enough time? | Honest workload, scheduling and access terms | Course/cohort detail |
| Why pay instead of using free material? | Demonstrated structure, teaching and support, when actually provided | Teaching and offering detail |
| What happens after I click or pay? | Accurate next-step explanation, access and purchase conditions | CTA destination and checkout |

Do not add an FAQ full of guessed questions simply to make the page longer.
Do not answer every purchasing objection in the hero. Distribute the argument across the funnel.

## Copy and CTA rules

- Write for the learner's desired progress, not the founder's platform architecture.
- Use concrete verbs and specific benefits. Remove generic praise and duplicate labels.
- Support claims with evidence near the claim. Do not invent outcomes, testimonials, urgency, scarcity, award affiliations or availability.
- Use one dominant action per decision point. Repeating Explore courses is coherent; adding three equally loud options everywhere is not.
- CTA wording must match the destination. A sample must open a sample. Coaching enquiry must not imply a confirmed booking.
- Reduce unnecessary account/forms effort before discovery. Keep necessary permissions and security.
- Do not use price discounts as a substitute for communicating value.
- Read copy aloud and check its logic before changing colour, decoration or animation.

## Current-page audit and next implementation priorities

The current prototype now has a clear broad structure, but these are the remaining commercial weaknesses:
- Competition marks cannot carry the whole value proposition. Supporting copy needs a clear learning benefit.
- A media placeholder reserves space but is not evidence yet. Final assets must show actual teaching.
- X+ is not coverage proof. Final count must come from covered offerings/competition records.
- Course suitability must become clear at the destination: outcome, level, syllabus, instructor, format and terms.
- Coaching and Arena should explain why someone would choose them, not merely announce two more products.
- Generic final CTA copy is less important than making the preceding offer and destination convincing.

Next editing pass: use this brief to rewrite the supporting copy and section transitions, then verify CTA destination consistency. Keep the motto, current owner-approved section order and member experience.
Product-fact gaps: actual first course inventory, confirmed course authors, deliverables, workload/access and purchase policies. These need business facts, not invented copy.
Real customer enquiries/sales conversations can later improve wording. Do not upload or expose private customer records or claim research that was not performed.

## Completion standard

A section earns its place by helping the visitor recognise fit, understand value, believe evidence, resolve an objection or take the next step.
Assess the complete path from traffic source through offering and purchase/enquiry, not homepage clicks alone.
Track qualified offer exploration and eventual purchase/enquiry quality when real measurement exists. Do not add analytics integrations or access production data under this document.
